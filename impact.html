Students Behind the Numbers

Reading After Sunset

With access to textbooks and notebooks, students at Ntaruka Fisher no longer share a single book across a bench. Reading happens after class—sometimes by lantern light.

Attendance That Holds

Before support, absences were common during fishing seasons. With uniforms and supplies provided, attendance stabilized across the term.

Academic Outcomes

  • Improved reading fluency in early grades
  • Reduced dropout risk among primary students
  • Increased classroom participation
  • Consistent access to learning materials
