Our Programs

Practical support. Real classrooms. Consistent presence.

Bring Them Back


Bring Them Back is Light Them Foundation’s commitment to students who disappeared from the classroom—not because they lacked ability, but because life pushed them out.

We identify children who have dropped out of school, listen to their stories, understand what went missing—whether it’s materials, fees, or family pressure—and respond with practical support. Then we walk with them back to school.
This program turns absence into return, hesitation into confidence, and lost time into a second chance at learning.

Sangira N’Abandi


Sangira N’Abandi is Light Them Foundation’s promise to students who are still in school, but quietly falling behind because they lack what others have.

We support learners who attend class every day yet struggle due to unpaid fees, missing materials, or conditions that make them feel different from their peers. By covering essential school costs and providing learning materials, we help them remain in school with dignity and confidence.

This program keeps students from slipping out of the system—so they don’t just stay in school, but truly belong there.

Round Tables


Round Tables is Light Them Foundation’s program for strengthening learning at the foundation level—Primary 4, 5, and 6—where habits, confidence, and understanding are built.

We bring students together in guided discussions around tables, creating space for questions, practice, and active learning. Alongside this, we provide improved learning materials, targeted seminars, and curriculum support that reinforce what students learn in class. Teachers are trained to use more effective, student-centered approaches, ensuring support continues beyond our sessions.

Progress is tracked through regular evaluations. What works is strengthened. What doesn’t is fixed.
The goal is simple: better learning, stronger teachers, and students who are prepared for the next stage of their education.
